The project

Aurion is a fictional luxury sports brand, created for a school project in which I had to design a product, its ads and its slogans. I imagined a range of high-quality table tennis paddles available in a choice of colors, and I produced a series of vertical Instagram ads to promote it.

The brief started with a question: what can luxury mean for a sports brand? Instead of spreading across a whole catalog, I focused on one offer and one niche: color customization. The idea is a premium product that people become attached to and want to collect in every color, a bit like the Stanley cup trend.

Design choices

Concept

Instead of the classic signs of luxury, I explored luxury as personal choice and attachment. The paddles are high-quality products, but the real offer is color: each customer picks the shade that feels like them and can collect more over time, a bit like the Stanley cup trend, where the color becomes part of the owner’s identity. This gave the brand one clear, focused idea, and every ad repeats it.

Visuals

To make the ads recognizable as one campaign, each one follows the same recipe: a hero paddle, a soft green backdrop and a scatter of bright orange balls. Staging the paddles like objects in a gallery, on podiums or in framed panels, gives them a premium feel, while the floating balls keep the energy playful and sporty. Each ad has one product, one headline and one button, so the message is understood in the time it takes to scroll past.

Colors

The brand identity rests mainly on a soft, fresh green. It evokes calm and the outdoors, and it echoes the green of a table tennis table. It’s also a neutral base that makes every product color stand out, now and in future shades. The yellow of the ping-pong balls is a recurring accent that adds energy.

Slogans

Every slogan plays with color: “Trouvez votre teinte”, “Exprimez votre jeu, exprimez vos couleurs” and “Jouez à votre couleur”. They are short and personal, and they speak directly to the customer’s own style. Titles are set in tall, bold, condensed capitals that are easy to read on a phone screen, with a few italic or yellow words for emphasis. The buttons use matching wording (“Découvrir la gamme”, “Adoptez votre style”, “Voir la collection”), which keeps the voice consistent.
