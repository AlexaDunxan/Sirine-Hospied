The project

Sweet Home is a fictional brand of baking subscription boxes, created as a design and branding exercise to learn how to build a strong visual identity. Each box is an all-in-one pastry kit with fresh ingredients, a recipe and professional tools. I designed the full identity, imagined the packaging and the range of products, and created a website concept to present them.

The brief targeted families and people who love baking as a hobby. The brand had to feel young, dynamic and fun while still looking like a top-quality product, and it had to sell an experience, not just a box. It needed a logo, a color and typography system, and a website design that guides visitors from discovery to subscribing.

Design choices

Logo

The logo is a flat splash shape with the brand name inside, like a drop of batter that has just landed. The splash conveys energy and fun, and keeping it flat and simple makes it feel modern and clean rather than messy. I created several versions (pink, beige, black, white and a mini version) so it stays readable on any background, down to favicon size.

Colors

The palette is inspired by sweets and food. Pink shades recall strawberries and frosting, while yellow and cream evoke vanilla custard and butter, which gives the brand its strawberry-and-cream feel. A deeper burnt rose, like jam or dark chocolate, adds contrast so the text stays readable. The mix is cheerful and young, but the soft, creamy tones also keep it feeling like quality and appetizing, not childish.

Typography

Lalezar is used for titles. Its heavy, rounded shapes echo the chunky lettering of the logo and stay punchy at large sizes, so headlines grab attention while staying easy to read. Lexend is used for subtitles and body text. It was designed for easy reading, which suits product details and forms, especially on a phone. Its clean shapes balance the expressiveness of Lalezar, so the pair feels playful without being tiring to read.

Products

Beyond the logo, I imagined the brand’s world: the packaging and the range it could sell. The boxes are shaped like a small bakery shop front, with a striped awning and a window, which reinforces the cozy “home” feeling. Each flavor has its own color and name (Strawberry, Rhubarb, Peach, Chocolate and Caramel), split between classics and seasonal boxes, so the range feels varied and gives customers a reason to come back.
